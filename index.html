I'm a Software Engineer with a Bachelor of Science in Mathematics from Universitas Andalas. For over five years in software-house environments I've specialized in ERP development with the Odoo framework — leading module design, data migration, multi-database server management, and end-to-end implementations that streamline real business workflows.

My backend core is Python and PostgreSQL, complemented by modern web development with JavaScript, React, and FastAPI. Outside of work, machine-learning forecasting is a personal interest: my Prophet-based forecasting app is an ongoing side project whose features I keep developing.

The mathematician in me never left: I've published research on rainbow connection numbers in graph theory, and I bring that same rigor — decompose, prove, optimize — to every engineering problem. Originally from Sungai Limau, West Sumatra; currently building things in Tangerang, Banten.
